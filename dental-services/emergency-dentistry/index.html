Emergency Dentistry in Payson, UT

When you need care right away, call us first.

Book an appointment(801) 465-3256
In pain right now? Call (801) 465-3256. We do our best to see emergencies the same day.

Dental emergencies rarely happen at a convenient time. If you're dealing with sudden pain, a broken tooth, or an injury, call us as soon as possible — we do our best to see emergency patients the same day.

Acting quickly can make a real difference in outcomes, especially for knocked-out or broken teeth.

Call Us Right Away If You Have

  • Severe or sudden tooth pain
  • A knocked-out, chipped, or broken tooth
  • Swelling in your face, gums, or jaw
  • A lost filling or crown
  • Bleeding that won't stop, or a dental injury from an accident

What to Do & What to Expect

  1. Call Us Immediately

    Reach us at (801) 465-3256 and describe what happened — we'll get you in as soon as we can.

  2. Knocked-Out Tooth?

    If possible, keep the tooth moist (in milk or held gently in place) and get to our office within 30-60 minutes for the best chance of saving it.

  3. Pain Relief & Diagnosis

    We'll assess the issue, manage your pain, and explain what treatment is needed.

  4. Treatment Plan

    Whether it's a filling, extraction, or root canal, we'll walk you through next steps clearly.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, uncontrolled bleeding, or a lost restoration all warrant a same-day call to our office.

What should I do if my tooth gets knocked out?

Handle it by the crown (not the root), rinse gently if dirty, and try to reinsert it or keep it in milk. Get to our office as quickly as possible — ideally within an hour.

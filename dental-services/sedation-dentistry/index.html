Sedation Dentistry in Payson, UT

A calmer, more comfortable way to get dental work done.

Book an appointment(801) 465-3256

Dental anxiety is common, and it shouldn't be a reason to put off care you need. Sedation options can help you feel calm and relaxed, whether you're anxious about a routine visit or facing a longer procedure.

Our team can walk you through the sedation options that fit your situation.

Sedation Dentistry May Help If

  • You feel anxious or fearful about dental visits
  • You have a sensitive gag reflex
  • You're having a longer or more complex procedure done
  • You have difficulty getting numb with local anesthesia alone
  • Past dental experiences have made you hesitant to seek care

What to Expect

  1. Consultation

    We discuss your anxiety level, medical history, and the procedure planned to recommend the right option.

  2. Sedation Options

    We'll explain the option we recommend and how to prepare for your visit.

  3. During Treatment

    You'll be monitored throughout your procedure for comfort and safety.

  4. Recovery

    Depending on the sedation used, you may need a driver to take you home afterward.

Frequently asked questions

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Sedation dentistry is widely used and considered safe for appropriately screened patients. We review your medical history beforehand to confirm it's a good fit.

Will I need someone to drive me home?

Depending on the type of sedation used, yes — we'll let you know in advance so you can plan accordingly.

Dental Services in Payson, UT

From routine cleanings to same-day emergencies, Farley Family Dental offers comprehensive care for every member of your family. Explore each service to learn what to expect.

Exams & Cleanings

Twice-yearly checkups and professional cleanings to keep small problems from becoming big ones.

Learn more

Fillings & Restorative

Tooth-colored fillings that repair cavities and minor damage without changing how your smile looks.

Learn more

Root Canals

Relieve infection-related tooth pain and save your natural tooth instead of losing it.

Crowns & Bridges

Restore a damaged tooth or replace a missing one with a natural-looking, durable crown or bridge.

Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

Safe, comfortable tooth removal and wisdom teeth evaluation when a tooth can't, or shouldn't, be saved.

Learn more

Dental Implants

A permanent, natural-feeling way to replace one or more missing teeth.

Learn more

Dentures & Partials

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers and professional teeth whitening to help you love the smile you see in the mirror.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners

Learn more

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, friendly dental care for kids, from their first tooth through their teenage years.

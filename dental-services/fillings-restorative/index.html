Fillings & Restorative Dentistry in Payson, UT

Repairing cavities and everyday wear, without the metal look.

Book an appointment(801) 465-3256

A cavity doesn't have to mean a big production. Most fillings are quick, comfortable, and — with today's tooth-colored composite materials — nearly invisible once placed.

Restorative dentistry is about more than filling holes: it's about stopping decay before it spreads and getting your bite back to normal as soon as possible.

Signs You May Need a Filling

  • Sensitivity to hot, cold, or sweet foods
  • A visible dark spot or pit on a tooth
  • A rough or sharp edge you can feel with your tongue
  • An old filling that's cracked, worn, or discolored
  • Pain when biting down in one specific spot

What to Expect

  1. Exam & Diagnosis

    We confirm the extent of decay with an exam and X-rays, then walk you through your options.

  2. Numbing & Prep

    The area is numbed, and any decayed tooth structure is carefully removed.

  3. Composite Filling Placement

    A tooth-colored material is shaped and hardened in layers to match your natural tooth.

  4. Bite Check

    We check your bite and polish the filling so it feels completely natural.

Frequently asked questions

Are composite fillings as strong as metal fillings?

For most cavities, tooth-colored composite fillings hold up very well and are the standard of care today. They also bond directly to the tooth, which can help preserve more natural tooth structure.

How long do fillings last?

Composite fillings typically last 7-10+ years with good oral hygiene, though this varies by location in the mouth and individual habits like grinding.

Will my insurance cover a filling?

Most dental plans cover part of the cost of a filling. Coverage varies by plan, and our team can help verify yours before treatment.

Dental Exams & Cleanings in Payson, UT

The simplest way to protect your smile for years to come.

Book an appointment(801) 465-3256

Most dental problems are far easier — and cheaper — to treat when they're caught early. A routine exam and cleaning twice a year is the single most effective thing you can do to avoid cavities, gum disease, and the kind of dental work nobody wants to schedule.

At Farley Family Dental, our exams combine a hands-on evaluation with digital X-rays when appropriate, so we can spot decay, bone loss, or other issues long before you'd feel any symptoms.

It's Probably Time for a Cleaning If

  • It's been six months (or longer) since your last visit
  • You notice bleeding when you brush or floss
  • You have visible plaque or tartar buildup
  • You've had persistent bad breath
  • You have a family history of gum disease

What to Expect

  1. Comprehensive Exam

    We check your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw for early warning signs of decay, gum disease, or wear.

  2. Digital X-Rays (as needed)

    Low-radiation imaging lets us see between teeth and below the gumline where problems often hide.

  3. Professional Cleaning

    Our hygienists remove plaque and tartar buildup that brushing at home can't reach, then polish your teeth.

  4. Personalized Care Plan

    We'll walk you through anything we found and what — if anything — needs attention next.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most patients do well with a cleaning every six months. If you have gum disease or other risk factors, we may recommend more frequent visits — we'll let you know at your appointment.

Does a cleaning hurt?

A standard cleaning is generally comfortable. If you have sensitive teeth or gums, let your hygienist know beforehand so they can adjust their approach.

What's the difference between a regular cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A regular cleaning addresses plaque and tartar above the gumline. A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) treats buildup below the gumline, usually for patients with early gum disease.

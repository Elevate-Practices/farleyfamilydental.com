Pediatric & Family Dentistry in Payson, UT

Making dental visits something kids don't dread.

Book an appointment(801) 465-3256

Good dental habits start early, and so does a comfortable relationship with the dentist. We aim to make every visit calm and reassuring, so kids grow up seeing the dentist as routine, not something to fear.

As a family practice, we're also glad to see the whole family in one place, on the same day when scheduling allows.

A Few Pediatric Dentistry Basics

  • The American Dental Association recommends a first dental visit by age 1 or within six months of the first tooth
  • Sealants can help protect kids' molars from cavities in hard-to-clean grooves
  • Fluoride treatments support stronger enamel during childhood development
  • Regular checkups help track jaw growth and bite development
  • Establishing a dental home early makes future visits easier for kids and parents alike

What to Expect

  1. A Friendly First Visit

    We keep first visits especially light and reassuring, focused on comfort over procedure.

  2. Gentle Exam & Cleaning

    We check for cavities and monitor development, cleaning teeth as age-appropriate.

  3. Preventive Care Discussion

    We'll talk through sealants, fluoride, and at-home habits that make the biggest difference.

  4. Building Comfort Over Time

    Each visit is an opportunity to build trust, especially for kids who start out nervous.

Frequently asked questions

When should my child first visit the dentist?

Most guidelines recommend a first visit by age 1, or within six months of the first tooth erupting — earlier than many parents expect.

Are dental sealants worth it for kids?

Sealants are a quick, painless way to protect the deep grooves of back teeth where cavities commonly start, and they're widely recommended by pediatric dental guidelines.

What if my child is anxious about the dentist?

Let us know ahead of time — we can adjust our approach, keep the visit shorter, and take things at your child's pace.
